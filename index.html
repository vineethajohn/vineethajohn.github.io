👩‍🏫 About Me

I am a High School Malayalam Teacher at Saint John's High School, Eravipuram — a school with over 85 years of educational heritage in Kollam. With 20+ years of teaching experience across Kerala and the UAE, I am passionate about nurturing students through language, literature and life skills.

Beyond the classroom, I believe in the power of compassion, environmental awareness and student-centred learning. This space documents my work, activities and reflections from the school that I am proud to serve.
